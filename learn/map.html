The AI Learning Map.

The whole field in nine branches, with a reading path for each one at your level, SCAIO's South Carolina primers, and a short check at the end.

Artificial intelligence is easier to learn when you can see the whole of it at once. This map divides the field into nine branches, from how machines learn to who builds the chips, and gives each one a path through three levels of depth. Pick the branch you need and read at the level that fits. No need to go in order.

Each branch also has a South Carolina layer: the SCAIO primers and Journal articles that apply the topic to this state. Those open right here on the page.

Click a branch name to jump to it. Click a circle to open or close it.
